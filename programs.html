Ages 1.5 - 3 Years

Toddler Program

A warm, nurturing Montessori environment where toddlers safely explore, discover, and build independence through purposeful play and hands-on learning.

Our Toddler Program is thoughtfully designed for children between 1.5 and 3 years of age — a stage filled with curiosity, rapid development, and a growing desire for independence.

The classroom environment is calm, organized, and beautifully prepared with child-sized materials that encourage movement, exploration, concentration, and self-confidence.

Children are gently guided through meaningful daily activities that help them develop language, coordination, social awareness, emotional security, and practical life skills at their own pace.

Practical Life Skills

  • Dressing and self-care routines
  • Hand washing and toileting independence
  • Food preparation and snack time
  • Cleaning and caring for the environment
  • Developing concentration and coordination

Early Development

  • Language-rich communication
  • Fine and gross motor development
  • Music, movement, and sensory activities
  • Social interaction and emotional growth
  • Exploration through hands-on experiences

Learning Through Discovery

Through observation and gentle guidance, teachers introduce materials and experiences that support each child’s natural development and growing independence.

Our toddlers learn in a positive, respectful, and non-competitive environment where they feel secure, confident, and joyful in their everyday learning.

"Education must begin at birth."

~ Maria Montessori

Ages 3 - 6 Years

Primary Program

A carefully prepared Montessori environment where children build independence, confidence, creativity, and a lifelong love for learning.

Our Montessori Primary Program serves children between the ages of 3 and 6 years during one of the most important stages of development.

The classroom is intentionally designed to encourage independence, curiosity, concentration, and joyful exploration. Children are free to choose purposeful activities that match their developmental needs and interests while being guided by trained Montessori educators.

Through hands-on learning and carefully designed materials, children gradually move from concrete experiences to deeper understanding and abstract thinking.

Core Learning Areas

  • Language and early literacy
  • Phonics, reading, and vocabulary
  • Hands-on mathematics
  • Sensorial exploration
  • Science and cultural studies

Practical & Creative Development

  • Practical life activities
  • Grace and courtesy lessons
  • Art, music, and creative expression
  • Fine motor and coordination skills
  • Independence and responsibility

Prepared Montessori Environment

Every material in the classroom is intentionally designed to encourage exploration, concentration, problem-solving, and independent learning.

Children learn through movement, repetition, observation, collaboration, and real-life experiences that help them build confidence and meaningful understanding.

Ages 6 - 12 Years

Elementary Program

A dynamic Montessori learning community where children become confident thinkers, responsible leaders, and independent lifelong learners.

Our Montessori Elementary Program supports children between the ages of 6 and 12 years through meaningful academic exploration, collaborative learning, and real-world experiences.

Students are encouraged to question, research, create, and discover through hands-on learning experiences that strengthen independence, responsibility, communication, and critical thinking.

The Montessori elementary environment inspires children to become active learners who are capable of managing their work, collaborating respectfully, and developing confidence in their own abilities.

Cosmic Education

At the heart of the Montessori Elementary curriculum is Cosmic Education — an interconnected approach that helps children understand the relationship between people, nature, science, culture, and the universe.

Through storytelling, exploration, and research, students develop curiosity, imagination, and a deeper understanding of the world around them.

Academic Exploration

  • Advanced reading and writing
  • Hands-on mathematics
  • Research and project-based learning
  • Science and experimentation
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving

Culture & Leadership

  • History and geography
  • Botany and zoology
  • Art and creative expression
  • Leadership and collaboration
  • Community responsibility

The Five Great Lessons

Montessori Elementary students are introduced to learning through inspiring stories that spark imagination and curiosity.

  • The Story of the Universe
  • The Coming of Life
  • The Coming of Humans
  • The Story of Writing
  • The Story of Numbers

Leadership Through Community

Mixed-age classrooms encourage collaboration, mentorship, empathy, and leadership. Younger students learn from older peers, while older students strengthen confidence and responsibility by guiding others.

This unique Montessori experience helps children grow into respectful, capable, and self-assured individuals prepared for future academic and life success.
